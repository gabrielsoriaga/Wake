LEARN THE STYLE

BEGINNER

SURFACE 180

Start between the wakes at a comfortable speed, with knees bent and the handle held low near your hips. Gently twist your hips and shoulders to slide the board around 180 degrees while keeping your upper body balanced over it. Keep the turn smooth, stay low, and look ahead as you settle into riding switch. Practice both directions and get comfortable riding switch before trying a spinning jump.

INTERMEDIATE

HEELSIDE BACKROLL

Try this only once you can do controlled heelside wake-to-wake jumps consistently; start with a coach and a wake size suited to your ability. Cut progressively toward the wake on your heels, keeping your knees bent and the handle close as you approach the top. At the lip, extend to pop and guide the roll with your head and back shoulder while keeping the handle close to your body. Spot the water as you rotate, land with soft knees, and stop if your approach or pop feels uncontrolled.

ADVANCED

TANTRUM

A tantrum is an inverted backflip, so learn it with a qualified coach after you have solid wake jumps and are comfortable with the setup and landing. Build a controlled heelside approach toward the wake, keeping the handle close and your body balanced. At the wake, pop upward first, then use your head and shoulders to initiate the backflip rather than throwing yourself early. Spot the landing, bring the board underneath you, and absorb the landing with bent knees; don’t attempt it alone or while fatigued.
