STEP 1

SET YOUR STANCE

Float on your back with the board across the rope. Bring your knees toward your chest, keep both arms straight, and hold the handle low.

LIFE VEST

A fitted watersports vest keeps you buoyant after a fall without restricting your arms.

HELMET

Essential at cable parks and around obstacles; choose a water-sports-rated, secure fit.

TOW CABLE

A non-stretch wake line and comfortable handle transfer a steady pull to the rider.

BOARD

Choose a board matched to your weight and ability, with fins and bindings set correctly.
